Cookie Bridge

This extension asks your Kiri instance which sites its installed plugins need cookies for, then — once you grant it access to a site from the toolbar popup — sends that site's cookies and User-Agent to Kiri automatically, so cookie-gated syncs work without pasting a cookie by hand.
